Spring Manufacturing · Inventory Module

Spring Inventory User Guide

How to use each screen, what the system does behind it, and how the application compares with the SOP & Process Flow (v1.0, 13 Oct 2026). Written for store, production, quality and dispatch staff, and for the managers who approve their work.

1. What the system does

The module follows every kilogram of wire from the supplier's truck to the customer. Wire arrives as bundles (raw material, counted in kg). It is issued to production, where it becomes a Production Lot of springs (work in progress, counted in nos). A lot moves through its routing: coiling, heat treatment, grinding, plating and so on. Some steps are done in-house and some by outside job workers. After final inspection the accepted springs become an FG Lot in FG Stores, ready to dispatch against the customer's schedule.

Every screen in the left sidebar is one step of that journey. The system proposes what to do next, checks each entry, and records every stock movement in one stock ledger, so any number on any screen can be traced back to the document that created it.

2. Rules that never change

Oldest stock first (FIFO)

Material issue, job-work send and dispatch always pick the oldest eligible bundle, lot or FG lot. A wrong scan is blocked and the screen names the one that should go first. Only an Admin can override, and must give a reason.

No negative stock

No bundle, lot or FG lot can go below zero. Entries above what is available are refused with the available figure in the message.

Held stock cannot move

Bundles in Quality Hold, anything Rejected and lots At Job Worker cannot be issued, sent or dispatched.

Nothing is edited or deleted

A saved transaction is permanent. Mistakes are corrected by a reversing transaction (Admin) or a stock adjustment, both with a reason, and both are kept in the history.

Numbers come from the system

Bundle, lot, GRN, MRN and every other document number is generated automatically, runs per financial year, and is never reused.

Reasons are mandatory

Every rejection, override, adjustment, scrap, return to stores and skipped job work needs a reason.

3. Users and sign-in

Sign in at the login page with your email and password. New users, and users whose password was reset, must choose their own password on first sign-in. After 5 wrong passwords the account is locked for 15 minutes. Use Sign out (top right) when you leave a shared terminal.

There are two roles today. Admin can do everything, including approvals, masters, adjustments and all reports. Floor users make the day-to-day entries. The menu only shows what your role may use, and the server refuses any action your role is not allowed, even from a direct link.

6. Step-by-step guide

Each stage lists who does it, where to find it in the sidebar, the steps, and what the system does.

6.1 Receive material against a PO (GRN)

Who: Store keeperScreen: Receipt & QC → GRN (Receipt)
  1. Select the open PO. The screen shows each PO line with ordered, received and balance quantity.
  2. Enter the supplier invoice no., invoice date (today or earlier, never a future date) and vehicle no.
  3. Enter the received quantity per PO line, then each bundle: weight (kg), Heat No. and test certificate reference. Grade and diameter come from the PO line.
  4. Optionally attach the scanned invoice or delivery challan (PDF, JPG, JPEG or PNG, up to 5 MB).
  5. Click Save GRN.
System: refuses a receipt above PO balance + tolerance % (set in Masters), and a line quantity that does not match its bundle weights. Each bundle is created in Quality Hold in the Incoming / QC area with inward date = GRN date (its FIFO and aging date). Ledger: T1.

Adding a PO (Admin)

  1. On the GRN screen open Add new PO. Store keepers do not see this.
  2. Optionally upload the scanned PO and click Read PO with AI. The form is filled from the document and a yellow box lists anything to check. Correct any field, tick "I have checked the values read by AI", then Confirm & save PO. (AI reading works only once an administrator has set it up; see section 8.)
  3. Otherwise type the PO no., choose the supplier (or "Add new supplier") and add the wire lines with ordered kg.
System: the PO date is always today. A PO cannot be back-dated or post-dated.

6.2 Incoming quality inspection (IQC) and bundle tags

Who: QC inspector records; Admin (Quality head) approvesScreen: Receipt & QC → IQC
  1. The pending list groups bundles by GRN. Pick one bundle, or record the same result for the whole GRN.
  2. Enter diameter, tensile, surface and certificate check, and the decision: Accept, Reject (reason required) or Accept under deviation.
  3. An Admin reviews the recorded result and clicks Approve.
  4. Print the bundle tag (Bundle No., grade, diameter, weight, Heat No., inward date and QR code) and fix it to the coil. Floor users can print a tag once; reprints are Admin-only.
System: recording a result does not move stock. On approval an accepted bundle becomes Available in RM Stores (T2); a rejected bundle goes to Rejected – RM (T3) and onto the Rejections list for return to supplier or deviation. A bundle is accepted or rejected whole.

6.3 Material requisition (MRN)

Who: Production supervisorScreen: Stores & Production → MRN
  1. Choose the Part No. and enter the planned number of springs.
  2. The system proposes the wire and required kg from the BOM. You may change the kg within the limit set in Masters (default ±10 %).
  3. Click Create MRN.
System: MRN status Open. No stock moves or is reserved. The MRN list shows the RM wire and the RM bundle numbers issued against each MRN, with search by MRN, bundle or wire code and filters by part and status.

6.4 Issue material to production (FIFO)

Who: Store keeperScreen: Stores & Production → Material Issue (FIFO)
  1. Open an Open or Partially Issued MRN. The screen shows the FIFO-suggested bundles of the matching grade and diameter.
  2. Scan or type each bundle tag and enter the kg issued from it (full or part), or use Add all FIFO-suggested bundles.
  3. Click Confirm & save issue.
System: a bundle that is not the oldest is blocked and the message names the bundle to use first. An Admin may override with a reason, which is logged. Each bundle's balance goes down (T5); a part-used bundle keeps its inward date. A Production Lot is created and linked to every bundle and kg. The MRN becomes Partially Issued or Closed. Every issue appears in Reports → Issue Register with the bundle balance left.

6.5 Return unused wire to stores

Who: Store keeperScreen: Stores & Production → Return to Stores
  1. Find the lot and bundle the wire came from. Only lots that have not started production are listed.
  2. Enter the return quantity (kg), choose Why returned? and add remarks if needed.
  3. Click Return to stores. The returns history below the form shows every return with quantity and reason.
System: the wire goes back to the same bundle with its original inward date (T6), so it keeps its FIFO place. The MRN status is recalculated.

6.6 Production and operation entry

Who: Production supervisorScreen: Stores & Production → Production / Operations

Each lot in the factory shows its current routing step. What you do depends on the step.

At an in-house step (coiling, grinding…)

  1. The card shows Planned, Processed so far and Pending. At coiling, planned is the MRN's planned springs for the wire this lot holds; the wire's maximum by weight is shown under it. At later steps, planned is the good pieces from the previous step.
  2. Enter Good and Rejected pieces. A rejected quantity needs a rejection reason. You may enter part of the quantity now and the rest later.
  3. Click Save entry. It stays disabled for 0 + 0, negative numbers, missing reasons, or a total above pending.
System: pending = planned − (all good + all rejected) at that step. When pending reaches 0 the lot moves to the next routing step carrying the total good pieces. Rejected pieces go to Rejected – WIP (T12). A double click or repeated submit is saved only once, and two people saving at once can never exceed the plan.
If coiling makes fewer springs than planned (end scrap, losses), record the shortfall as Rejected with a reason so the step completes.

At a job-work step (heat treatment, plating…)

  1. Send to Job Work opens the JW Out Challan screen (section 6.7).
  2. Skip Job Work asks for a reason, then Confirm skip. The lot moves to the next routing step, or to Final Inspection when the job-work step was the last one.
System: a skip moves no stock. It is kept on the lot as a skipped operation with its reason, plus an audit record.

6.7 Job work: send out, receive back, return QC

Who: Dispatch clerk sends; store keeper receives; QC inspector checks returnsScreens: Job Work → JW Out Challan · JW In Receipt

Send out

  1. On JW Out Challan, tick the lots to send. They are listed oldest-first, with a suggested kg.
  2. Choose the job worker, enter the Vehicle No. (required) and, if needed, an expected return date. It defaults to challan date + lead time and can never exceed the vendor's maximum return days.
  3. Click Create challan, then Print, or download it as Excel or PDF from the challan list.
System: FIFO by lot start date (Admin override with reason). The whole lot moves to At Job Worker – vendor (T8); it is still our stock.

Receive back: one challan, many return batches

Vendors often return material in several deliveries. Each delivery is a return batch recorded against the original challan; the challan is never duplicated.

  1. On Job Work → JW In Receipt, find the challan and click Open challan. The top shows Dispatched, Returned, Outstanding with vendor, Pending QC, QC passed and QC rejected.
  2. Click Record Partial Return on the lot and enter this delivery: Received good, Rejected by vendor (reason required), Short / lost, the receipt date, and optionally the vendor's DC no. and remarks.
  3. Click Save return batch. The batch gets its own number (JWI-…) and appears under Return batches, ready for QC at once.
  4. Repeat for every later delivery. Earlier batches are never changed.
  5. An Admin can close a challan with a shortage, with a reason; whatever is still with the vendor is written off as short.
System: outstanding with vendor = dispatched − returned − rejected by vendor − short. A batch can never exceed what is outstanding, the receipt date cannot be in the future or before the challan date, and the same delivery cannot be saved twice (a double click or two people saving at once). The received pieces of each batch go to JW Return QC as their own lot (T9), keeping the lot's bundles and start date, while the rest stays At Job Worker. Anything still with the vendor past the expected date is Overdue.

Return QC, batch by batch

  1. On the challan page, click Start QC (or Continue QC) on a batch.
  2. Enter QC passed and QC rejected pieces (reason required for rejects) and any remarks. You may inspect part of the batch now and the rest later.
  3. Click Save QC. The batch shows Awaiting QC, QC In Progress or QC Complete, with the last inspector and date; every inspection is kept in QC history.
System: passed + rejected can never exceed the pieces waiting in that batch. Only QC-passed pieces go back in-house at the next routing step (T10); rejected pieces go to Rejected – WIP for a rework, return-to-vendor or scrap decision on Rejections / Disposal. The challan status is worked out from its quantities: Awaiting Vendor Return → Partially Returned → QC Pending / QC In Progress → Rejections Pending Disposal → Completed, with indicators such as "2,500 pcs awaiting QC · 1,580 pcs outstanding with vendor".

6.8 Final inspection and finished goods

Who: QC inspector records; Admin (Quality head) approvesScreen: FG & Dispatch → Final Inspection
  1. Step 1 – Lots ready for final inspection: the inspector enters accepted and rejected nos (covering the whole lot), a reason for rejects and the test results, then clicks Record.
  2. Step 2 – Awaiting Quality Admin approval: an Admin checks the result and clicks Approve → FG.
System: nothing moves when the result is recorded. On approval the accepted pieces become an FG Lot (FG-YYMM-####) in FG Stores, dated on the approval day (T11). Rejected pieces go to Rejected – FG, and the production lot closes. FG lots are listed in FG & Dispatch → FG Stock (FG Stores).

6.9 Dispatch and customer returns

Who: Dispatch clerkScreen: FG & Dispatch → Dispatch
  1. Due schedule lines show customer, Part No., quantity and due date, with the FIFO proposal of FG lots.
  2. Enter the quantity, invoice no., vehicle no. and packing details, then save. Print the dispatch note from the list.
  3. For a return, use Customer Return Note with the original dispatch no., FG lot, quantity and reason.
System: dispatch cannot exceed FG available, nor the schedule balance unless an Admin ticks "Approve over-dispatch". FG goes out oldest-first (T15); the schedule becomes Partially Dispatched or Dispatched. A return can only come from a dispatch that carried that FG lot, up to what was dispatched, and goes to Rejected – FG (T16).

6.10 Rejections and disposal

Who: Admin (Quality head)Screen: Rejections → Rejections / Disposal

Every rejection, from any stage, appears here with its stage, quantity, reason and status. Filter by stage, disposal or rejection no.

6.11 Corrections: stock adjustment and reversal (Admin)

Screens: Admin → Stock Adjustment · Transaction Reversal

Stock Adjustment changes a bundle's kg by a +/- amount with a reason (T17), for example after a physical count. Transaction Reversal posts the exact opposite of a wrong GRN, issue, dispatch or adjustment, with a reason. A transaction can be reversed only once, and an issue cannot be reversed once its lot has started production.

8. Masters and settings

Masters → Masters holds the reference data: wire items (grade, diameter), parts with net weight per piece and scrap allowance %, BOM / routing steps (in-house or job work, default job worker, lead days), suppliers, job workers (with maximum return days), customers, locations, purchase orders, customer schedules and reason codes for each stage (IQC, WIP, JW, FQI, Customer, Adjustment, Return). Floor users can view masters; Admins can add and change them.

Admin settings on the same screen: GRN tolerance %, aging buckets, Quality Hold alert days, JW due-alert days and the MRN kg edit limit.

AI reading of PO documents is switched on by setting ANTHROPIC_API_KEY on the server (optionally PO_OCR_MODEL). When it is on, uploaded PO documents are sent to Anthropic's API to be read. Nothing is saved until a person confirms the values.
